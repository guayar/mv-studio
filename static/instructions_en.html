MV Studio – how to work with chunks

0. New song: upload and split into chunks

  1. New project (top bar) – a name is enough.
  2. Tab Song & split: choose the file and click Upload song.
  3. Paste the lyrics: one line = one sung phrase. Mark sections with a separate line, e.g. [Verse 1].
  4. Set the maximum chunk length and click Analyse and split. Whisper listens to the song and finds the phrase times (a few minutes).
  5. In "Phrases with times" listen (▶) and fix times where needed, then Save times and split again.
  6. "Proposed chunks" shows the split: whole phrases, none is cut, gaps without vocals become separate chunks. Insert into chunk plan moves it to the list on the left.

1. Choosing the chunk you work on

  • Pick the project from the list at the top.
  • Chunks are in the left column, ordered by song time. Click a chunk – it gets an orange border and the whole right side is about it.
  • The dot next to the name: green = accepted, blue = rendered and waiting for your verdict, yellow = rendering, red = rejected or error, grey = nothing yet.
  • New chunk: + Add chunk below the list. Delete: Delete chunk in the bar at the top of the chunk (video files stay on disk).
Chunks form a chain. The calculations and render of the next chunk unlock only when the previous one is accepted, because the film length and the extend base depend on it.

2. Working on a chunk, step by step

  1. Film preview – "▶ Film up to here" shows the picture with music as it currently is before this chunk.
  2. Chunk audio – ▶ Chunk plays its part of the song; words light up while playing.
  3. Calculations – read the warnings (e.g. a phrase at the very end of the clip). If needed, change "Ordered seconds" and save.
  4. First chunk only: Make still from reference in card "1. Chunk".
  5. Prompt – click Insert template and replace the words in CAPITALS, or type/paste your own, then Check prompt. Red messages must be fixed, yellow ones are notes.
  6. Render – "Show commands (dry run)" starts nothing. Render starts the render (one at a time; the app refuses when ComfyUI is busy).

3. Reviewing and accepting a generated chunk

After the render, the new version appears at the bottom, in card 5. Versions and approval, and the accept buttons also appear in the bar at the top of the chunk.

  • Show clip – just the raw clip.
  • Assemble with music and show – joins the whole film so far with this version and the real song, playing from 4 s before the seam.
  • Accept – this version becomes part of the film.
  • Reject – the app asks for a reason and logs it (Rules button). The file stays on disk.
  • Use this prompt – puts that version's prompt into the editor so you can fix it and render the next version.
  • Delete version – removes the version from the app; the video file stays on disk.

4. Joining with the rest of the film

  • There is no separate joining step. Accepting a chunk puts it into the film.
  • The whole film is written on every "Assemble with music and show": projects/<project>/film/film_<version>.mp4.
  • Changing your mind: accept another version of the same chunk and the film switches to it.
